## Important limits

The UI renders wedge bonds but does not derive R/S or E/Z configurations. Aromatic bonds are stored and rendered but conversion between aromatic and Kekulé forms is unavailable. The local mass/valence code is intentionally narrow; a formula or mass for unusual chemistry may be unavailable or require independent verification. MOL V2000 and SVG exports can lose data; the app presents a loss warning before export. The `.mols` format is the only format intended to preserve every field currently in the model.

## Verified library facts

[RDKit](https://github.com/rdkit/rdkit/) is BSD-3-Clause and has documented descriptor and molecular rendering APIs; it is not installed locally. [Open Babel](https://openbabel.org/docs/UseTheLibrary/intro.html) documents C++ library use and GPL terms, and its [format overview](https://openbabel.org/docs/FileFormats/Overview.html) lists broad format support. [IUPAC](https://iupac.org/what-we-do/periodic-table-of-elements/) publishes the periodic table and standard atomic weights. These references informed the integration plan; they do not imply those libraries are active in this build.
